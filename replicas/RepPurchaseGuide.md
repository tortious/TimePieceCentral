# 🥷 Replica Luxury Watch Buying Guide (≤ $600)

A practical playbook for sourcing and buying replica luxury watches under $600 from Trusted Dealers (TDs). Includes a full QC checklist.

---

## 1. Know the Tiers
- **$50–150**  
  Low-tier reps. Cheap quartz/auto, poor finishing.  
- **$150–300**  
  Mid-tier reps. Decent finishing, NH35/Miyota autos, sapphire crystal.  
- **$300–600**  
  High-tier “super reps.” Solid cases, sapphire w/ AR, ETA clones / Seiko / TMI / hi-beats.

## 3. Step-by-Step Playbook

### Step 1. Pick Your Watch & Factory
- Decide on model (Rolex Sub, Omega Seamaster, Cartier Santos, etc.).  
- Identify the best **factory** (e.g., Clean, VSF, ZF, ARF, BP).  

---

### Step 2. Contact the TD
Example message:  Hi, I’m interested in the [watch model + factory + version]. Can you confirm stock and price?

---

### Step 3. Payment
- Common: Wise, Revolut, Western Union, Crypto (BTC/USDT).  
- Rare: PayPal (F&F). Avoid random PayPal emails.  

---

### Step 4. Receive QC Pics
Dealer sends photos/videos of your actual watch.  

---

### Step 5. Approve or Reject
- Approve if satisfied.  
- If flaws, reject politely:  Thank you, but the datewheel alignment looks off. Could you please source another?

---

### Step 6. Shipping
- Expect “triangle shipping” (China → EU/HK → US).  
- Timeline: 2–4 weeks. Tracking updates after customs.  

---

### Step 7. Inspect Upon Arrival
- Weight, bracelet fit, movement winding, clasp, lume.  
- Compare against genuine reference pics.  

---

### Step 8. Optional Mods
- Waterproofing.  
- Movement regulation.  
- Crystal or bracelet swaps.  
- Optional — most wear straight out of the box.  

---

## 4. QC Checklist ✅
When you receive QC pics/videos, **verify before approving:**

- [ ] **Dial text/alignment** → Check logo (Rolex coronet, Omega print) is sharp & centered.  
- [ ] **Datewheel font & position** → Numbers centered, no misprint.  
- [ ] **Hands alignment** → No floating/misaligned hands.  
- [ ] **Indices & markers** → Even, no crooked plots or lume misfill.  
- [ ] **Bracelet/end link fit** → Flush with lugs, no big gaps.  
- [ ] **Bezel insert** → Pearl at 12 is centered, markers evenly spaced.  
- [ ] **Crystal clarity/AR** → No haze, rainbow effect if AR coated.  
- [ ] **Caseback engraving** → Correct spelling, not sloppy.  
- [ ] **Movement rotor** (if visible) → Clean finish, not scratched.  
- [ ] **Overall finish** → No obvious scratches, dents, or dust under crystal.  

---

## 5. Pro Tips
- Start with **Rolex Sub, Omega Seamaster, Cartier Santos** — best value/least QC headaches.  
- Don’t spend max budget on first rep — test with $300–400 range first.  
- Save QC pics for resale or future reference.  
- Manage expectations: **good reps look/feel real but aren’t gen under loupe.**
